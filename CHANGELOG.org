#+title: yt changelog

* Unreleased
** After M7
- Channel playlists: =O= on a video (or in the channels list) lists the
  playlists its channel made; =RET= shows a playlist's videos in playlist
  order, refetched whole.  =+= saves a playlist in yt and =-= forgets it;
  =C-c y P= lists the saved playlists with their unwatched counts.  The
  cache schema is now version 3, with a playlists table.
- =C-c y RET= opens the subscription feed too, every key of =yt-map= has a
  label for which-key ("subscriptions", "channels", …), and
  =yt-subscriptions= is another name for =yt=, so the main view is easy to
  find.
** M7: thumbnails, diagnostics and documentation
- =yt-thumbs.el=: thumbnails from i.ytimg.com, fetched with curl without
  cookies, four at a time, once per video, through a =.part= file; the feed
  shows them in a first column (=t i=, or =yt-feed-thumbnails=) and asks only
  for the rows about to be seen.
- =yt-doctor= now also reports yt-dlp's JS runtimes, the mpv version, curl,
  the player in use, =DISPLAY=, the cache (mode, videos, watched, channels)
  and the thumbnail cache, in sections.
- README: installation (Home Manager module, overlay, straight.el), the
  dedicated profile, all keys, the sway window rule, options, privacy and
  known limitations.
- 5 new tests (142 in total).
** M6: completion, embark, marginalia and Nix packaging
- =yt-select.el=: =C-c y f= chooses a video of the feed with completion
  (category =yt-video=, annotated with channel, age and duration, optionally
  grouped by channel) and plays it.
- With embark, every video key acts on a =yt-video= candidate, and on the
  video at point in yt buffers.  With marginalia, the annotations are
  registered for the category.
- =packages.default= builds yt as an Emacs package with autoloads;
  =overlays.default= adds =yt= to every Emacs package set; the Home Manager
  module gained =programs.yt.installElisp= and now enables =programs.mpv=.
- New checks: the tests with byte-compiled code, and the package build.
- Fixed: two runs finding the session expired at nearly the same moment
  could export the cookies twice.
- 6 new tests (137 in total).
** M5: channels, uploads, search, info and playlist
- =yt-channels.el=: =C-c y c= (=l= in the feed) lists the subscribed channels
  with the number of unwatched feed videos and their last upload; =RET=
  shows a channel's uploads, =/= filters, =b= browses.  The list is cached
  for a day.
- Channel uploads (=RET= in the channels list, =o= on any video) show in
  =yt-feed-mode=, cached and refreshed like the subscription feed.
- =C-c y s= searches YouTube; the query is kept out of the logs.
- =yt-info.el=: =i= shows a video at once, then its description (with
  links), chapters as buttons that play from their start, tags, views,
  likes and the exact upload time.  At most two such fetches run at once.
- =C-c y l= (=P l=) shows mpv's playlist: =RET= plays an entry, =d=
  removes it, =g= refreshes; it follows mpv as files start and end.
- The video keys (=RET=, =v=, =m=, =a=, =i=, =o=, =w=, =u=, =b=, =y=, =Y=)
  work in the feed, info and playlist buffers alike.
- Fixed: an exact upload time stayed marked approximate after merging.
- 11 new tests (131 in total).
** M4: mpv controlled from Emacs
- =yt-mpv.el=: one idle mpv, started on first use on the selected frame's
  display and driven over JSON IPC through a socket in the private runtime
  directory.  Pending commands wait for the connection; a closed socket is
  reconnected (up to 3 times) and the properties observed again.
- Playlist entries are followed by id: a video counts as watched once
  =yt-player-watched-threshold= (0.9) of it has played or it plays to the
  end, never after an error; otherwise its position is saved, and =RET=
  resumes there (=C-u RET= plays from the start).  A playback error
  resolves the video again, once, and resumes where it failed.
- =m= plays in mpv, =a= queues (in order, also on a region); =auto= now
  picks mpv when it is installed.
- Player keys in =yt-map= and under =P=: =SPC= pause, arrows seek 5 s and
  60 s, =[= / =]= speed, === normal speed, =n= / =p= next and previous,
  =S= stop, =Q= quit; they repeat without the prefix.
- =yt-player-mode= shows =▶ Title 12:34/45:00 ×1.25= in the mode line.
- When mpv exits or Emacs is killed, the position is saved and the socket
  removed.
- =homeModules.default= (=programs.yt.enable=) installs mpv and the
  cookie exporter.
- 17 new tests (120 in total) against a fake mpv IPC server.
** M3: playback in VLC
- =yt-player.el=: the player backend interface, =yt-play= (=RET=),
  =yt-play-vlc= (=v=), =yt-play-url= (=C-c y u=), =yt-player-stop= (=S=) and
  =yt-player-show-log= (=!=).  Streams are resolved with yt-dlp and the
  signed-in cookies, retried once with format =b= when the format is not
  available, and handed to the player only after YouTube's =available_at=
  time, so that fetching them does not fail with 403.
- =yt-vlc.el=: plays the separate video and audio streams in VLC, on the
  display of the selected frame; a new video replaces the running VLC.
  Videos count as watched when VLC starts.
- Stream URLs are redacted from every log.
- =yt-player-backend= (=auto= picks VLC until the mpv backend exists),
  =yt-player-format=, =yt-vlc-program= and =yt-vlc-extra-args=.
- 15 new tests (103 in total), with a fake VLC.
** M2: cache and subscription feed
- =yt-cache.el=: SQLite cache (mode 0600, WAL, busy timeout, schema
  version) of allowlisted video fields, the order of each feed, watched
  state and resume positions; =yt-mark-watched= with
  =yt-watched-changed-functions=, and =yt-forget-history=.  Shorts inherit
  the time of the video before them; a more exact time always wins.
- =yt-feed.el=: =M-x yt= shows the cached feed at once and then refreshes it
  asynchronously, signing in first if there are no cookies.  A refresh stops
  after =yt-feed-stop-after-known= cached videos in a row, except the first
  of a session; =G= cancels and refetches everything.  Point and each
  window's position stay on the same video.
- Header line with the sign-in state, last update, filters and count.
- Filters that never refetch: a query (=s=, =/=; words, =@channel=,
  =!exclude=), a channel (=c=, =C= clears) and toggles for watched videos,
  Shorts and upcoming streams (=t w=, =t s=, =t u=).
- =w= / =u= mark watched or not (on the region too), =b= browses, =y= / =Y=
  copy the URL or "Title — URL".
- The sandbox is now default Emacs plus yt: it starts with =-q
  --no-site-file= and its only config is the tracked =sandbox/init.el=.
- 29 new tests (88 in total).
** M1: sign-in and yt-dlp backend
- =yt-process.el=: asynchronous runner with separate standard error, JSON,
  streamed NDJSON with early stop, timeouts, cancellation and one run per
  key.
- =yt-video.el=: the =yt-video= record built from yt-dlp JSON, and age and
  duration formatting.
- =yt-ytdlp.el=: yt-dlp argument lists ending in =--=, an ordered error
  classifier, a denylist for =yt-ytdlp-extra-args= and a version check.
- =yt-auth.el=: =yt-login= (choose a Chromium profile, export its
  youtube.com cookies, verify), =yt-check-auth=, =yt-logout=; every yt-dlp
  run gets a private 0600 cookie copy whose rotated cookies are written back
  atomically; an expired session is re-exported once, with concurrent runs
  sharing the export; back-off after bot checks and rate limits.
- =yt-doctor.el=: =yt-doctor= (programs, environment, directories, cookie
  file modes and counts, sign-in state) and =yt-debug-fetch-feed=.
- The sandbox generates autoloads from the working tree.
- 59 tests using scripted fake yt-dlp and exporter programs.

** M0: project skeleton
- Nix flake with the test Emacs, dev shell, =nix run .#test=,
  =nix run .#sandbox=, =nix flake check= checks and the =yt-export-cookies=
  exporter.
- Makefile targets that run only with the flake's Emacs, in an isolated
  HOME/XDG environment.
- =yt.el= with private state and runtime directories, =yt-map= and a stub
  =yt= command.
- Tests for directory privacy, test isolation and the fixture leak guard.
